# Output

Return the structured verdict:

- `facts_in_context`: the NUMBERS of the facts the context states, or states an
  equivalent of.
- `facts_not_in_context`: the NUMBERS of the facts the context does not state.
  Every number listed above must appear in exactly one of the two lists. Do not
  invent a number, do not put a number in both lists, and do not return text in
  either list.
- `notes`: at most two sentences for a human reviewer.
