# Output

Return the structured verdict:

- `gold_facts_covered`: the NUMBERS of the gold facts the answer states, or
  states an equivalent of.
- `gold_facts_missed`: the NUMBERS of the remaining gold facts.
  Every number from 1 to {{ gold_facts | length }} must appear in exactly one of
  the two lists. Do not invent a number outside that range, do not put a number
  in both lists, and do not return text in either list.
- `wrong_claims`: claims in the answer that contradict a gold fact or the
  retrieved context.
- `fabricated_claims`: specific claims in the answer supported by neither the
  retrieved context nor the gold facts.
- `stance_errors`: places where the answer asserts as true something the
  documents negate, dispute, or merely allege, or reports as negated something
  the documents establish.
- `notes`: at most three sentences for a human reviewer.
