JOINT PRETRIAL STATEMENT (excerpt). Harriet Adams v. City of Clifton, Docket No. CLF-2025-114. Filed November 3, 2025.

4. Valuation. The parties do not agree on the fair market value of the 0.62-acre parcel at 118 Harlow Street condemned by the City of Clifton.

5. Plaintiff Harriet Adams offers the appraisal of Dolores Vance, MAI, dated September 8, 2025, which concludes that the fair market value of the parcel as of the July 1, 2024 date of taking was $1,480,000, derived from three closed comparable sales within one mile of the subject.

6. The City of Clifton offers the appraisal of Terrence Baptiste, dated October 2, 2025, which concludes that the fair market value of the same parcel as of July 1, 2024 was approximately $865,000 after a deduction of roughly $210,000 for wetlands remediation that Vance did not apply.
