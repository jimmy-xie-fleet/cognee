MINUTES OF THE CLIFTON PLANNING BOARD, regular meeting of April 9, 2025.

The Clifton Planning Board voted 5-2 to recommend approval of site plan application SP-2025-19 for the 14-unit building at 300 Harlow Street, subject to the conditions in the engineer's memorandum of March 28, 2025.

Mr. Smith, appearing on behalf of the applicant, stated that the applicant would fund the full cost of the Harlow Street sidewalk extension. The applicant of record is Smith Holdings LLC, which acquired 300 Harlow Street from the City of Clifton in 2022.

The Board noted that its recommendation is advisory only and that the Clifton Municipal Council must act on the application within 45 days. The City of Clifton has not issued any building permit for 300 Harlow Street.
