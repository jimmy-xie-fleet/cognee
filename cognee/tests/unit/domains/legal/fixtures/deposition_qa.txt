DEPOSITION OF RAYMOND ELLERBEE, taken May 22, 2025. Ellerbee v. Northgate Transit Authority, Docket No. NT-2025-0442.

Q. How far was the bus from the crosswalk when you first saw it?

A. Maybe forty or fifty feet. I'm not certain. It was raining hard and I had my hood up.

Q. Where were you standing when the bus started to move?

A. On the northeast corner, by the mailbox.

Q. Did you see the driver look to his right before the bus started to move?

A. No. I was watching the crosswalk, not the cab.
