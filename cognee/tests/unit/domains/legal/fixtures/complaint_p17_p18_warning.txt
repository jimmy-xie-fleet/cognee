IN THE UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE DISTRICT OF MINNESOTA

AMARA OKAFOR, Plaintiff, v. MERIDIAN LOGISTICS, INC., Defendant.

Case No. 26-cv-00814. COMPLAINT AND DEMAND FOR JURY TRIAL. Filed March 10, 2026.

17. On February 20, 2025, Plaintiff Amara Okafor reported to Meridian's Director of Human Resources that her supervisor had instructed warehouse staff to falsify driver hours-of-service logs.

18. On March 3, 2025, eleven days after that report, Meridian Logistics, Inc. issued Ms. Okafor a written warning for insubordination, the first negative entry in her six-year personnel file. Plaintiff alleges that the written warning was issued in retaliation for her February 20, 2025 report and for no legitimate business reason.
